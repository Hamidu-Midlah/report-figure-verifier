# AI Adoption in Financial Services — Draft Chapter (Demo)

In 2026, 36% of surveyed firms report scaling AI across their organisation,
up from 32% in 2024. Combined adoption (scaling plus piloting) now stands at
60% of firms.

The share of firms piloting AI has declined to 24% in 2026, consistent with a
shift from experimentation to production. Only 6% of firms expect no change
to their AI strategy.

Adoption remains uneven across markets: less mature markets account for 38%
of the sample, while more mature markets account for 73%.

Regional investment tells a similar story. North America leads with USD 4.2
billion in AI investment, followed by Asia-Pacific at USD 3.1 billion and
Europe at USD 2.8 billion.

According to a separate industry estimate by an external consultancy, global
AI spending will reach USD 200 billion by 2028.
